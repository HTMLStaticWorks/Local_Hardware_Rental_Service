Our Story

Your Trusted Rental Partner

We believe that high-quality tools and equipment are the foundation of any successful construction project.

Born from Reliability

Founded in 2010 by a team of construction and tool specialists, ToolRent started as a small local rental yard and grew into a premier destination for professional contractors and DIYers.

Our mission is to provide reliable, commercial-grade equipment, alongside professional customer support, while assisting clients with safe tool operations and safety guidelines. We inspect and service every tool before it ever leaves our warehouse.
